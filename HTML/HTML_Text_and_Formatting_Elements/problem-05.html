Science Notes

This page contains important notes and formulas from Science, including Chemistry, Physics, and Biology.

Chemistry

Chemistry is the study of matter, its properties, composition, and chemical reactions.

Important Chemical Formulas

Water: H2O

Carbon Dioxide: CO2

Oxygen: O2

Sulfuric Acid: H2SO4

Sodium Chloride: NaCl

H2O is the chemical formula for water.

Physics

Physics is the study of matter, energy, motion, and the forces acting on objects.

Scientific Expressions

Area of a square: m2

Volume of a cube: m3

Einstein's equation: E = mc2

Scientific power: 103 = 1000

Energy can be represented by the famous equation E = mc2.

Biology

Biology is the scientific study of living organisms and their life processes.

Important Biological Terms

Cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life.

DNA carries genetic information in living organisms.

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants prepare their food using sunlight.
