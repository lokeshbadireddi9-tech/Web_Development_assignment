India Launches New Satellite for Weather Monitoring

India has successfully launched a new satellite designed to improve weather forecasting and climate monitoring. The satellite was launched from the Satish Dhawan Space Centre.

The launch took place on 28 August 2026 and was carried out successfully by the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO). The satellite will provide important weather data to help predict storms and other severe weather conditions.

Scientists said that the new satellite will help improve weather predictions across India. This mission is an important step towards better weather forecasting and public safety.
