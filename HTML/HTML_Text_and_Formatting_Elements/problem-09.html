Student Terms & Rules

This page contains important rules and notices that every student is expected to follow.

General Rules

Students must carry their college ID card while on the campus.

Students must maintain proper discipline inside classrooms and other college areas.

Students are expected to respect teachers, staff members, and fellow students.

Attendance

Students must maintain the minimum required attendance according to college regulations.

Attendance shortage may affect eligibility for examinations.

Students should regularly attend classes and avoid unnecessary absence.

Examination

Students must carry their valid ID card and examination admit card to the examination hall.

Students must report to the examination hall at least 15 minutes before the examination.

Electronic devices and unauthorized materials are not allowed during examinations.

Assignment Submission

All assignments must be submitted before the given deadline.

Late submissions may receive reduced marks or may not be accepted.

Assignment Deadline: 30 September 2026
Students should keep a backup of their submitted work.

Discipline

Students must not damage college property or disturb academic activities.

Serious violations of college rules may result in disciplinary action.

Students should maintain a respectful and positive learning environment.

Important Notice

Every student is responsible for following the rules and instructions issued by the college.

Rules may be updated when necessary, so students should regularly check official college notices.
